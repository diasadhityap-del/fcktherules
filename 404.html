Football. Streets. Born to Disobey.

Tentang kami

FvcktheRules

Soccer culture. Street attitude. Born to Disobey.

FvcktheRules was born from a simple belief: football is more than a game.

It lives in the streets, in the concrete, in the late-night conversations, in the shirts we wear, the colors we carry, and the loyalty we refuse to hide. Football becomes part of who we are — an identity shaped by passion, belonging, rivalry, pride, and the culture that exists far beyond the pitch.

Founded in 2024, FvcktheRules is built for those who carry that culture everywhere they go.

We are inspired by the streets where football has always felt raw, spontaneous, and real. Where a ball can turn an empty space into a pitch, where a shirt can represent a lifetime of loyalty, and where football is not something you simply watch — it is something you live.

Born to Disobey

To question the rules.
To reject the ordinary.
To create your own identity.
To wear what you believe in.
To stand for what matters to you.

Every piece is created as an expression of that mindset — blending football culture with streetwear and an attitude that refuses to be defined by convention.

But FvcktheRules doesn't stop at clothing. We are building a football culture ecosystem — a space where football, fashion, stories, opinions, and people come together.

The Ecosystem

News
Football news from Indonesia and around the world. Transfers, matches, clubs, players, competitions — presented from a perspective that goes beyond the scoreline.
Culture
Football doesn't exist inside ninety minutes. Terraces, jerseys, music, street football, fashion, communities, cities, rivalries, and the people who make football what it is.
Opinion
Space for different voices and conversations that challenge the usual narrative. No need to agree. Just bring something worth talking about.
Journal
Long-form writing about football, people, places, history, and identity. Not just what happened, but why it matters.
Zine
Independent football publishing. Limited digital and physical zines featuring photography, essays, interviews, illustrations, and archives.
E-Book
Longer stories deserve more space. Digital books on football culture, individuals, communities, clubs, cities, and movements that deserve to be documented.
Discussion
Football belongs to everyone. A space for supporters, collectors, players, creators, writers, and designers. Because sometimes the best part of football isn't the answer. It's the conversation.

More than a clothing brand

FvcktheRules is a media platform, publishing space, community, and clothing label built around football culture.

The clothing is what you wear.
The stories are what you read.
The discussions are what you join.
The culture is what you live.

We want to build a place where football doesn't have to be separated from everyday life. Where a jersey can be fashion. Where a photograph can be history. Where an opinion can start a conversation. Where a zine can become an archive. Where a community can become a movement. And where getting involved doesn't require permission.

For the ones who never leave football at the pitch.
For the ones who wear their passion every day.
For the ones who choose their own path.
For the ones who were never made to fit in.
